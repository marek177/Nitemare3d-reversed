## Behavior model
Calibration clears0109, executes setup, samples center and passes status to8ECA. Even if it reports and disables, caller proceeds to capabilities query, quotient/clamp, final sample and another status check. Non-null output is scaled regardless of newly disabled state. There is no local enabled recheck between these steps. Disabling affects later guarded callers, not an implicit abort in this routine.
