## Behavior model
Normalize once, derive orientation selectors, then load sine-like and cosine-like values. Full valid input range-360..719 produces final0..359. Inputs outside this range may remain invalid; the routine does not iterate until in range. Retain separate literal tables: cosine quarter60=511 differs from reversed sine quarter30=512 as noted by prior static evidence. Derived error terms are initialized from the selected table values; later movement may mutate them separately.
