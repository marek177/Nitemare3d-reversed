## Behavior model

Return normally.8F86 does not check enabled again after8ECA; it scales the local sample.963E checked enabled once at entry and likewise continues with returned record, event processing, elapsed threshold and prior-button state updates. A later963E invocation with disabled flag skips input. Same-invocation continuation is proven under inert dialog semantics; actual UI can have separate effects.
