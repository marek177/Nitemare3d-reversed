## Behavior model
D6C6 performs one selected API call then stores/returns its32-bit value without conversion, clamp, subtraction or wrap correction. State7 sequence is read1→0C42(value1)→read2→963E(value2)→95AE. Consumer clobbering of return registers cannot replace the second read. Arbitrary pairs, includingFFFFFFFF→0, pass through unchanged; handling elapsed time belongs to consumers, not this wrapper.
