## Behavior model
With existing calibration, get sample `(rawXWORD,rawYWORD,rawZWORD,buttonsWORD)`. For each X/Y compute true difference of two unsigned16-bit values, yielding signed32 range-65535..65535. Divide by positive stored scale with signed truncation, then retain low16 bits in output. Copy buttons0..3 into output+4, preserving bits4..7 and all of output+5. Consumer963E later interprets output axes as signedWORD; therefore large quotients can wrap before that consumer. This is local arithmetic behavior, not a demonstrated real calibrated-device condition.

## Implementation-ready findings
