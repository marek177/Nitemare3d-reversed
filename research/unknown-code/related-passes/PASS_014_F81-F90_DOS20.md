## Specific boundary cautions
- F81 0x3D9B is an early return, not an end of the function; primary final RETF 0x3ED4.
- F83 0x3F7C is likewise an early return; final RETF 0x40DA.
- F86 near call at 0x426C to 0x4162 has PUSH CS; must simulate far-style stack frame.
- F89 near call at 0x4431 to 0x430A similarly has PUSH CS.
- Returned source offsets and segment constants require relocation audit. Do not claim Win16 equivalents.
- Missing single-byte ranges are 0x90 NOPs in candidate boundaries, check exact source bytes before concluding no hidden entry.
